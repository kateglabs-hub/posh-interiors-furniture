## Chosen Direction: Contemporary African Atelier

### Design Movement
Contemporary African modernism blended with editorial art direction and boutique hospitality design.

### Core Principles
1. **Material-led luxury:** Ivory plaster, warm timber, smoked glass, linen, and brushed brass create a tactile sense of quality.
2. **Editorial asymmetry:** Layouts use off-axis composition, oversized type, and image-led storytelling instead of uniform centered cards.
3. **Quiet confidence:** Copy is concise, assured, and specific; visual restraint replaces loud decoration.
4. **Human-scale conversion:** Every aspirational moment is paired with an easy action—WhatsApp, call, quote, or consultation.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is built around warm ivory and charcoal to make the site feel like a sunlit Nairobi residence or a premium materials library. A muted ochre-brass accent signals craftsmanship and Kenyan warmth without looking ornamental. Deep espresso and olive-black provide grounding for high-impact sections.

### Layout Paradigm
A scrolling editorial journey with alternating wide image bands, offset text columns, horizontal category rails, and occasional full-bleed moments. Content should feel discovered rather than stacked into a standard template. On mobile, the same rhythm collapses into generous vertical chapters with clear hierarchy.

### Signature Elements
- Thin brass rules and numbered section labels such as `01 / The Studio`.
- Soft arch and framed-image motifs inspired by doorways and architectural elevations.
- A floating WhatsApp enquiry tab that remains discreet but visible.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like handling a premium material sample: immediate, tactile, and restrained. Links reveal with a brass underline; cards lift or shift image crops subtly; mobile menus open as a calm full-height panel. Conversion actions remain obvious without becoming visually aggressive.

### Animation
Use 180–280ms ease-out transitions for buttons, nav states, and image hover crops. Section content may fade and rise slightly on entry, with 40–70ms stagger between grouped items. Avoid continuous motion and parallax-heavy effects. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling entrance transforms and hover zoom.

### Typography System
Use **Cormorant Garamond** for display headings, pull quotes, and editorial statements; use **Manrope** for navigation, body copy, labels, and controls. Headlines use high contrast, generous tracking on uppercase eyebrow labels, and restrained line lengths. Body copy stays at a readable 16–18px with relaxed line-height.

### Brand Essence
**Positioning:** A premium Kenyan interior design and furniture studio creating sophisticated, functional, and timeless spaces through design, décor, furniture, and fittings.

**Personality:** Considered, warm, exacting.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are editorial and evocative. CTAs are direct and low-pressure. Microcopy is helpful, calm, and locally grounded.

Example lines:
- “A considered interior begins with a better conversation.”
- “Tell us what you’re imagining. We’ll shape the next step.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a bold, text-free monogram symbol built from two interlocking architectural `P` forms, suggesting a plan drawing, doorway, or paired spaces. The wordmark is set separately in uppercase Manrope with wide tracking; the mark should remain recognizable at favicon size.

### Signature Brand Color
**Posh Brass — `#B08B57`**, a muted, earthy brass that reads as crafted and ownable rather than shiny gold.

### File-Level Reminder
Every CSS/component/page file should preserve the Contemporary African Atelier direction: warm material palette, editorial asymmetry, restrained brass accents, Cormorant Garamond + Manrope typography, and conversion actions that feel calm and premium.
